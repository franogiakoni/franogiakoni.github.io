# Design

Visual system of frano-giakoni CV site (`index.html`, `assets/styles.css`, `assets/app.js`).

## World

Graphite night with a gold accent, drawn from the athletics track: lane lines, a finish straight and arcs from Santiago. Formal, academic, editorial; one dark theme by design (portfolio read at a desk or on a phone, photos and covers carry the light).

| `--bg-2` | `#1c1c1a` | Cards, detail panels, inputs |

| `--l-salud` | `#d9a089` | Research line: physical activity & health |

Type: Archivo (variable width/weight) for everything; display semi-expanded (`font-stretch` 104–108%), weight 600, tracking −0.025 to −0.03em. Archivo Narrow with tabular numerals for data (years, citations, km, dates): a scoreboard voice, never monospace. Solid colours only, no gradient text.

Motion: one easing `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`. Content-led moments: hero intro and lede highlight, publication deck (coverflow, drag), track lanes drawing, word-by-word section titles, staggered lists, count-up metrics and growing bars, plan bars, map arcs with travelling dots, chronological map tour, cover clip reveals, header that hides on scroll down, sliding nav indicator. No custom cursor, no ambient glows, no hover spotlights, no 3D card tilt, no magnetic buttons. All respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Components

- **Section**: max 1320px, gutter `clamp(16px,4vw,56px)`, top hairline, title `.section-title` (no eyebrow labels).
- **Photography**: duotone (grayscale + gold soft-light), full colour on hover/focus.
- **Controls**: buttons, chips, language toggle; radius 4px, 1px `--line` border (printed-programme feel).
- **Lists over cards**: research lines, profiles, press and side notes are ruled rows/columns (hairline or 2px line-colour rule), not boxed cards. Panels (`--bg-2`, radius 4px) only for the map card, Scholar CTA and lightbox.
- **Data graphics**: citations bar chart, plans Gantt (2023–2031), world map (d3-geo, vendored).

## Page order

Hero (round portrait with spinning ring, name, roles, socials, publication deck) → Scholar metrics + academic profiles (Scholar, Scopus, ResearchGate, ORCID) → El Libro Blanco (3D cover, “first Chilean sport management book”) → Development plans (covers + 2023–2031 Gantt) → Research lines (3 ruled columns) → Publications (Scholar CTA + filterable list, 10 per page) → Career (two-column list by blocks with the small purpose quote; the owner considers it the core of the CV) → Talks map + event archive (album lightbox) → In the media (clippings index) → Beyond the lecture hall → Contact.

Decided against (owner feedback): hero cutout photo over the deck, photo carousel strip, affiliation logo wall, career timeline graphic, large statement band, keyword marquee, golden cursor ring, totals of press appearances and spokesperson ranking.

## Content rules

Bilingual via `<span lang="es">` / `<span lang="en">` pairs toggled by `html[data-lang]`. Public channels: email (frano.giakoni@unab.cl), LinkedIn, Instagram, X and Google Scholar. Publications and metrics come from `assets/publications.js` and `assets/scholar-stats.js`, regenerated by `tools/update_scholar.py`; never invent metrics.
