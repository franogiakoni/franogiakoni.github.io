## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS (no build step), bilingual ES/EN with a client-side toggle; deployable to GitHub Pages, Netlify or Vercel. Chosen because the content is a single-person CV that must be cheap to update and host.

## Users

- Academic committees (hiring, grants, postdoc, doctoral review) scanning credentials quickly.
- Research collaborators (national and international) looking for fit and contact.
- Industry and sport organizations (clubs, federations, companies) evaluating expertise for partnerships.
- Students and thesis candidates looking for a supervisor.

Personal academic-professional website and CV of Dr. Frano Vilim Giakoni Ramírez. It presents his trajectory, research, publications and roles so each audience can judge credibility and reach him. Success: a visitor leaves convinced of his academic standing and knows how to contact him.

Combines academic leadership (UNAB program director, journal director) with field management experience.
